How to Play Indian Rummy

A comprehensive, accessible guide to the rules, card combinations, and winning declarations in 13-card Indian Rummy.

1. Objective of the Game

In 13-card Indian Rummy, each player is dealt 13 cards. The main objective is to arrange all 13 cards in your hand into valid combinations: Sequences and Sets.

To make a valid declaration and win the game, your hand must satisfy the declaration rules with 0 unmatched (loose) cards.

2. Pure Sequence (Mandatory)

A Pure Sequence is a group of 3 or more consecutive cards of the SAME suit formed without using any joker as a substitute.

  • Mandatory Requirement: You MUST have at least one Pure Sequence to make a valid declaration and win the round. Without a pure sequence, any declaration is invalid and incurs penalty points.
  • Example: 4♥ 5♥ 6♥ (Three consecutive hearts)
  • Extended Example: 10♠ J♠ Q♠ K♠ (Four consecutive spades)
  • Wild Card in Natural Role: If a card has the same rank as the wild joker (for instance, 7♦ when 7 is the wild joker), it can still form a natural pure sequence in its own suit (6♦ 7♦ 8♦). Printed Jokers can never be part of a Pure Sequence.
  • Ace Position: Aces can be used as low or high cards (e.g. A♣ 2♣ 3♣ or Q♣ K♣ A♣), but round-the-corner sequences (such as K-A-2) are not valid.

3. Impure Sequence

An Impure Sequence is a group of 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit where one or more cards are substituted by a Joker (Printed Joker or Wild Joker).

  • Requirement: A winning hand must have at least two sequences in total. The first sequence must be Pure; the second sequence may be either Pure or Impure.
  • Example with Printed Joker: 7♥ 8♥ [PJ] Joker (Joker replaces the 9♥)
  • Example with Wild Joker: If 3 is the open Wild Joker, then 5♠ 3♦ (Wild) 7♠ forms a valid impure sequence of 5-6-7 of Spades.

4. Sets

A Set is a group of 3 or 4 cards of the SAME rank but belonging to DIFFERENT suits.

  • Valid Set Example (3 suits): 7♠ 7♥ 7♣
  • Valid Set Example (4 suits): K♠ K♥ K♣ K♦
  • Set with Joker: You may also use a wild card or printed joker in a set, e.g. 9♠ 9♥ [PJ] Joker
  • Duplicate Suit Rule: A set cannot contain two cards of the exact same suit. For example, 7♠ 7♠ 7♥ is an invalid set.

5. Jokers (Printed and Wild Jokers)

Jokers play a vital role in Indian Rummy by substituting for any missing card to complete impure sequences and sets.

  • Printed Joker: Standard jokers included in each 52-card deck with a printed clown illustration. They can replace any card in impure sequences and sets.
  • Open / Wild Joker: At the start of the game, one random card is cut from the deck and placed face-up as the Wild Joker. All cards of that rank across all four suits become Wild Jokers for that deal!
  • Cut Joker Exception: If the cut card happens to be a Printed Joker, the Ace of all suits acts as the Wild Joker for that round.

6. How to Declare & Win

To win a hand, all 13 cards must be arranged in valid groups according to these rules:

  • Rule 1 — At least 2 Sequences: You must have a minimum of two sequences.
  • Rule 2 — Mandatory Pure Sequence: At least one of your sequences must be Pure (no jokers).
  • Rule 3 — Second Sequence: The second sequence can be pure or impure.
  • Rule 4 — Remaining Cards: All other cards must be arranged in valid sequences or sets (e.g. 4-3-3-3 or 4-4-5 card groupings).
  • Rule 5 — Zero Unmatched Cards: You must have 0 unmatched cards left in your hand when you discard your 14th card to the finish/declare slot.
Winning Hand Example: Pure Sequence (4♥ 5♥ 6♥) + Impure Sequence (10♠ J♠ Joker) + Set (8♥ 8♣ 8♦) + Set (Q♠ Q♥ Q♦ Q♣) = 13 cards validly grouped!
